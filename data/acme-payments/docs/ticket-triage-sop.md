# Acme Payments ticket triage SOP

## Queues
general (unsorted), onboarding (KYB documents and applications), payouts (payout timing and holds), disputes (chargebacks), risk-limits (limit changes), compliance (sanctions and fraud alerts).

## Priorities
p1: platform outage or fraud in progress. p2: money blocked for a high-risk merchant, compliance alerts, or amounts above 10,000 USD. p3: standard requests. p4: information only.

## Sanctions alerts
Tickets about a merchant with a potential sanctions match go to the compliance queue at p2. Never tell the merchant that screening is in progress.

## Resolving tickets
Only resolve a ticket after the merchant has confirmed the issue is fixed. Add a note explaining the resolution.
