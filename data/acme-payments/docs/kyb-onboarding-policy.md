# Acme Payments merchant onboarding (KYB) policy

## Required documents for sole traders
A sole trader must provide a government-issued photo ID, a proof of address dated within the last 3 months (utility bill or bank letter) and a recent business bank statement showing the trading name.

## Required documents for limited companies
A limited company must provide its certificate of incorporation, the register of beneficial owners holding 25% or more, and a government-issued photo ID for every beneficial owner and director.

## Pending applications
While KYB status is pending, card acceptance is allowed but payouts stay on hold until every missing document is received and verified. Send a reminder after 5 business days. Applications still incomplete after 30 days are declined automatically.

## Risk ratings
Merchants are rated low, medium or high risk at onboarding. High-risk merchants need enhanced due diligence by the Compliance team before payouts are released.
