# Acme Payments payouts and processing limits

## Payout schedules
Low-risk merchants are paid daily. Medium-risk merchants are paid weekly, every Monday. High-risk merchants are paid weekly after a 7-day rolling reserve.

## Payout holds
Payouts are held when KYB is pending, when a sanctions screening alert is under review, or when the merchant's chargeback ratio is above 1% over the last 30 days.

## Limit increases
A low-risk merchant with at least 6 months of processing history can have its monthly limit raised up to 2x the current limit, approved by the Risk team. Anything above 2x needs a full credit review. Support agents never change limits themselves: they route the request to the risk-limits queue.
