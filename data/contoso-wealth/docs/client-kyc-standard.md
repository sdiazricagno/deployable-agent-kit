# Contoso Wealth client KYC standard

## Periodic review cadence
Individual and joint accounts are refreshed every 3 years. Trusts and other entities are refreshed every year. When a review date passes, KYC status becomes expired.

## Expired KYC
An account with expired KYC is restricted to sell-only: no new purchases and no outgoing transfers until the refresh is completed by the kyc-ops team.

## Documents for trusts
Onboarding a trust needs a certified copy of the trust deed, the list of trustees and beneficiaries, a photo ID for each trustee, and a source of wealth statement.

## Address changes
Address changes need a proof of address dated within the last 90 days, submitted through the secure client portal. Never accept an address change by email or phone.
