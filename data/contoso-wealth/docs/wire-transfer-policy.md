# Contoso Wealth wire transfer policy

## Compliance holds
Outgoing wires above 50,000 USD to a high-risk jurisdiction are held for compliance review. The review target is 2 business days.

## Callback verification
A first wire above 10,000 USD to a new beneficiary needs a phone callback to the number on file before release.

## Cut-off times
Domestic wires received before 4:00 pm ET are sent the same day. International wires must be received before 2:00 pm ET.
